# 🚨 Fix: Contributions Not Showing (0 vs Actual 695)

## ⚠️ Problem
Your profile shows **0 total contributions** but you actually have **695 contributions**.

## ✅ Solution - Enable Activity Overview

### **CRITICAL: Do This Now!**

1. **Go to Settings:**
   - Click your profile picture (top right)
   - Select **Settings** → **Public profile**
   - OR direct link: https://github.com/settings/profile

2. **Find "Contributions & Activity" Section:**
   - Scroll down to find this section
   - Look for "**Activity overview**" option

3. **ENABLE These Options:**
   - ✅ **"Show an overview of your activity on your profile"**
   - ✅ Keep **"Make profile private and hide activity"** → UNCHECKED
   - ✅ Save changes

4. **Verify Email Settings:**
   - Go to: https://github.com/settings/emails
   - Primary email must be ✅ **Verified**
   - ✅ **"Keep my email addresses private"** → UNCHECKED

---

## 📊 Why This Matters

When Activity Overview is disabled:
- ❌ Contribution count shows 0
- ❌ Contribution calendar is hidden
- ❌ Activity timeline is hidden
- ❌ Stats cards won't load data

When Activity Overview is enabled:
- ✅ Shows actual 695 contributions
- ✅ Contribution calendar displays
- ✅ Activity graph updates
- ✅ Stats cards show real numbers
